AppStage Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

AppStage ("the app") is a dashboard for Google Play Console data, published by FidovaSoft. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. The short version: your data never leaves your device except to talk directly to Google's APIs.

Data the app stores

  • Service account credentials you paste during setup are stored only on your device. They are used exclusively to authenticate directly against Google APIs (androidpublisher.googleapis.com, playdeveloperreporting.googleapis.com, storage.googleapis.com, oauth2.googleapis.com).
  • App data (release states, vitals, reviews, statistics) fetched from Google is cached on your device to power the dashboard, widgets and notifications.
  • Settings (theme, language, templates) are stored on your device.

Data we collect

None. AppStage has no backend servers, no analytics, no trackers and no advertising SDKs. We cannot see your credentials, your apps or your statistics.

Third parties

  • Google APIs: the app communicates directly with Google using the credentials you provide, under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing processes Pro subscription payments; we receive no payment details.
  • On-device translation (ML Kit) runs locally; review text is not sent to any server by the app.

Data deletion

Removing an account inside the app, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored data. Remember to also revoke the service account's access in Play Console and Google Cloud if you stop using it.
